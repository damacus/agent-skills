# Prepare Hard News

Help the user enter the conversation clear and honest. Do not soften the news
until its facts, impact, uncertainty, and required outcome are understood.

## Gather What Matters

Ask only for missing information that changes the message:

- what happened and what is confirmed versus inferred;
- who is affected and how;
- what accountability belongs to the speaker or team;
- what remains unknown and when it will be known;
- the proposed path forward; and
- what decision or support the conversation needs.

When helpful, ask the user for their natural opening and identify hedging,
blame shifting, minimization, or promises the evidence cannot support.

## Prepare the Conversation

Provide:

1. the news in one direct sentence;
2. a short opening that leads with the news;
3. a plain-language explanation of cause, impact, and uncertainty;
4. the concrete response plan, owners, and next update;
5. likely reactions and grounded responses; and
6. specific phrases or evasions to avoid.

Take accountability without inventing fault. Do not over-apologize, speculate,
or promise a date or outcome that has not been established.

This skill prepares or drafts communication. Never send a message, schedule a
meeting, or notify another person without explicit approval.
